```
Bowerbird: Screenshots and Folders for Posts
```

**Summary** (from the manifest, 111 of 132 characters)

```
Make clean screenshots of posts on x.com and save posts into your own folders. Everything stays on your device.
```

**Description** (paste as is; no word is repeated more than 5 times, which the store's spam rule checks)

```
Bowerbird adds one small button under each post on x.com. Use it to make a clean picture of what you see, or to keep it in folders you make yourself.

Screenshots
• Pick Light, Dim or Dark. Bowerbird draws a tidy image: the name, the text, the pictures, any quote and the date, without the buttons around them.
• The image is copied, ready to paste, and saved as a PNG in your Downloads folder. You can turn either one off in Settings.
• Options: show the reply, repost, like and view counts, and add padding on a soft background.
• Images are made at twice the normal size, so they stay sharp.

Folders
• Click Save post to keep something for later, or open the folder list to file it in one or more places. Type a name to make a new folder.
• Click the toolbar button to see everything you saved. Search by words, names or @handles. Rename, merge and delete folders and remove items, all with Undo.
• Export everything to a file, and import it again on another computer.

Private by design
• Everything stays in your browser on your computer. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• There is no server and no remote code. Pictures load from X's image server only to show them.
• It runs only on x.com and twitter.com, and reads only what you click.

Keyboard friendly
• Tab to the button, then use the arrow keys, Enter and Esc to move through the menus.

Bowerbird is an independent tool. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with X Corp.
```

**Category:** Social Media & Networking. If the dashboard does not list it, pick Tools.

**Language:** English
